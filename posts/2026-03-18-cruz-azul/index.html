Cruz Azul Dominates CONCACAF Champions Cup With Stunning Comeback Victory

March 18, 2026 12:44 Trend Insights

When a team scores two goals in the final ten minutes to turn a deficit into a road victory, you know something special is unfolding. Cruz Azul did exactly that in the first leg of their 2026 CONCACAF Champions Cup Round of 16 series against CF Monterrey, and the reigning champions are showing no signs of slowing down.

Key Takeaways

  • Cruz Azul rallied from 2-1 down to win 3-2 away at Monterrey in the first leg
  • Gonzalo Piovi equalized via penalty in the 84th minute; Nicolas Ibanez scored the winner in the 90th
  • The second leg takes place at Estadio Cuauhtemoc in Puebla, Mexico
  • Cruz Azul also signed Argentine striker Nicolas Ibanez from Tigres ahead of the transfer deadline
  • The winner on aggregate advances to the CONCACAF Champions Cup Quarterfinals

Cruz Azul’s Dramatic First-Leg Comeback Against Monterrey

The first leg of this Round of 16 clash at Monterrey’s home ground looked like it was slipping away from La Maquina. Roberto de la Rosa scored both goals for Rayados, putting the hosts in a commanding 2-1 position deep into the second half.

But Cruz Azul had other plans.

In the 84th minute, Gonzalo Piovi stepped up and converted a penalty kick to level the score at 2-2. Just six minutes later, Nicolas Ibanez delivered a stunning goal in the 90th minute to complete the comeback and hand Cruz Azul a crucial 3-2 away victory.

The result was historic for multiple reasons. According to CONCACAF, the loss snapped several impressive streaks for Monterrey:

  • Unbeaten home record in first legs – gone
  • Five consecutive home wins in the Champions Cup – ended
  • Five straight first-leg home clean sheets – shattered

It marked only the ninth time in CONCACAF Champions Cup history that a Liga MX club has lost a first-leg match at home in any stage (excluding Finals). And Monterrey became just the second team, alongside Deportivo Oro in 1963, to score two goals at home in a first leg and still lose.

So what does history say about teams in Monterrey’s position? The odds are not favorable, but this series is far from over.

The Second Leg: What’s at Stake at Estadio Cuauhtemoc

The second leg was set for Tuesday at Estadio Cuauhtemoc in Puebla, Mexico, with Cruz Azul holding a 3-2 aggregate advantage heading into the match.

Here is a breakdown of the scenarios:

ScenarioResult
Cruz Azul wins or drawsCruz Azul advances to Quarterfinals
Monterrey wins by 1 goalAway goals serve as first tiebreaker
Monterrey wins by 2+ goalsMonterrey advances on aggregate
Tied on aggregate after away goalsExtra time and penalty shootout

Away goals serve as the first tiebreaker if the series is level on aggregate. If that does not break the tie, extra time and a penalty shootout will determine the winner.

Cruz Azul entered the second leg as the reigning Champions Cup winners, having previously won the tournament by defeating the Vancouver Whitecaps. Their pedigree in this competition is formidable – the club is a six-time champion of CONCACAF and nine-time Liga MX champion.

But what about their squad depth heading into the crucial stretch?

Nicolas Ibanez Transfer: A Deadline Day Gamble That Paid Off

One of the most significant developments for Cruz Azul came off the pitch. As the Mexican transfer window closed on Monday at 7:00 p.m. ET, the club raced to finalize a deal for Nicolas Ibanez, the 31-year-old Argentine striker from Tigres UANL.

The move made perfect sense for both sides:

  • Ibanez had been removed from Tigres’ first-team roster and relegated to the U21 squad, making his departure inevitable
  • Cruz Azul entered deadline day thin at center forward, creating an urgent need for a proven striker
  • According to ESPN, Cruz Azul’s front office submitted a formal offer late Sunday night to Tigres in San Nicolas de los Garza

Ibanez’s Track Record in Mexican Football

The Argentine forward’s career numbers in Mexico speak for themselves. Across 141 matches with Atletico San Luis, Pachuca, and Tigres, Ibanez recorded:

  • 31 goals
  • 10 assists
  • Three titles: Clausura 2023, Campeon de Campeones 2022-23, and the 2023 Campeones Cup

Despite his impressive resume, Ibanez had struggled to secure consistent minutes with Tigres since 2023, which opened the door for this move.

Cruz Azul had all eight of its “non-Mexican-trained” roster spots available, meaning registering Ibanez would pose no issues for head coach Nicolas Larcamon.

The timing could not have been better. Ibanez scored the dramatic 90th-minute winner in the first leg against Monterrey, proving he can deliver in high-pressure moments. Having a striker of his caliber available for the remainder of the Champions Cup campaign significantly strengthens La Maquina’s title defense.

Cruz Azul’s Liga MX Form: A Balancing Act

While the CONCACAF Champions Cup has grabbed headlines, Cruz Azul also has domestic commitments to manage. The club faced Santos Laguna and Pumas UNAM in Liga MX action during the same period.

The match against Pumas on March 14 saw a competitive battle, with Pumas managing to come back and secure a draw against Cruz Azul. Balancing Liga MX fixtures with continental competition is a challenge every successful Mexican club must navigate, and it adds context to the squad depth concerns that drove the Ibanez signing.

Looking ahead, Cruz Azul has also been drawn into Leagues Cup 2026, where they will face Chicago Fire FC, Necaxa, and Santos Laguna in the group stage. The busy schedule means squad rotation and depth will be critical in the months ahead.

What This Means for CONCACAF Football

Cruz Azul’s run in the 2026 Champions Cup is more than just a club story. It speaks to the evolving competitiveness of CONCACAF’s premier club competition.

Consider these facts:

  • Cruz Azul advanced 8-0 on aggregate in a previous round, demonstrating their capacity for dominance
  • Their dramatic comeback against Monterrey shows they can also win in adversity
  • The away goals rule adds tactical complexity that rewards attacking football on the road

For fans in the United States, these matches matter. CONCACAF Champions Cup fixtures are available on platforms like Fubo, FS1, TUDN, and ViX, making it easy to follow the action. CBS Sports also provides coverage of key Liga MX and CONCACAF matches.

Common Misconceptions

Many casual fans assume Mexican club football is a step below European leagues in quality. But the level of tactical sophistication, the intensity of rivalries like Cruz Azul vs. Monterrey, and the sheer drama of late-game comebacks rival anything you will see in the Champions League. Cruz Azul’s current form is a prime example of why CONCACAF football deserves more attention from a global audience.

Looking Ahead: Can Cruz Azul Defend Their Crown?

With a 3-2 aggregate lead heading into the second leg, Cruz Azul is well-positioned to advance to the Quarterfinals. The addition of Nicolas Ibanez addresses a key squad weakness, and the team’s ability to win under pressure bodes well for the knockout rounds ahead.

The reigning champions have shown they can dominate (8-0 aggregate in earlier rounds) and grind out results (3-2 comeback at Monterrey). That versatility is what separates good teams from great ones.

As the 2026 CONCACAF Champions Cup progresses, all eyes will be on whether La Maquina can go back-to-back. With the talent, tactical acumen, and fighting spirit they have displayed so far, dismissing them would be a mistake.
