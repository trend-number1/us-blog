Eric Roberts Breaks Silence: Surprising Truth About Julia and Emma

April 17, 2026 12:03 Trend Insights

At 69 years old, Eric Roberts is finally doing what tabloids swore he never would — talking openly about his family. And the story he’s telling isn’t the one gossip columns have been selling for decades.

In a string of April 2026 podcast appearances, the veteran actor dismantled long-running narratives about a bitter rift with his Oscar-winning sister and a fractured bond with his famous daughter. The truth, he says, is messier and more human than the headlines suggest.

Quick Summary: Eric Roberts, 69, publicly shut down rumors of a feud with sister Julia Roberts, calling her “a cool chick” he likes and respects. He also addressed his “complicated” relationship with daughter Emma Roberts, admitting he was emotionally unavailable during her early childhood. A 1983 Bob Fosse film is the reason he’s still acting today.

Who Is Eric Roberts? The Hollywood Survivor You Already Know

Eric Roberts isn’t just “Julia Roberts’ brother” or “Emma Roberts’ dad” — he’s one of the most prolific working actors in Hollywood. But even he admits there was a moment he thought it was all over.

In his recent podcast interviews, Eric revealed that he believed his acting career was finished at age 27. A serious car accident left him fearing he would never work again. What brought him back? A single phone call about Bob Fosse’s final film.

That 1983 film — Star 80, Fosse’s last directorial work — became the lifeline that kept Eric in the industry. He has since built a filmography of hundreds of credits, making him one of the busiest character actors alive.

Why this matters: His comeback story underscores a theme Eric keeps returning to in 2026 — resilience, regret, and second chances. And those themes run straight through his answers about family.

The Julia Roberts “Feud” That Never Existed

For years, entertainment outlets framed Eric and Julia Roberts as estranged siblings locked in a quiet Hollywood cold war. Eric says that narrative is flat-out wrong.

Speaking on the It Happened in Hollywood podcast on April 14, 2026, Eric told host Seth Abramovitch:

  • “It’s not true — it never was true. We’re fine.”
  • “We have disagreements, as people do, but we like and respect each other very much.”
  • “She’s a wonderful actor, she’s a wonderful human… a cool chick and I like her.”

He also praised the 58-year-old Pretty Woman star as a “wonderful mother” to her three children, calling motherhood “the hardest job there is.”

Where Did the Rift Rumor Even Come From?

Much of the speculation traces back to a 2018 Vanity Fair interview in which Eric claimed that neither Julia nor his daughter Emma would be famous without him. That quote — which Eric has since called “asinine,” “unfortunate,” and “untrue” — became the foundation for nearly a decade of feud stories.

Eric now says the siblings rarely talk about each other publicly for a simple reason: the press keeps trying to turn them into a contest. Since they’re never up for the same roles, he notes, there’s no actual professional competition between them.

Here’s the twist — the most enduring celebrity sibling “feud” of the 2010s may have been manufactured almost entirely by one regretted quote and a press ecosystem hungry for conflict.

Eric Roberts and Emma Roberts: A Father-Daughter Story Rewritten

If the Julia storyline is about absence of conflict, the Emma storyline is about presence of honesty. And this is where Eric’s 2026 media tour gets most revealing.

On the Really Famous With Kara Mayer Robinson podcast, Eric was asked point-blank whether he and Emma Roberts have a “complicated” relationship. His response reframes the entire narrative:

“It’s only complicated through outside eyes because they don’t get any of it. So, they have their own take, whatever that is. I’m so proud of my kid. She didn’t start off as a great actor, but she became a great actor. She’s fabulous.”

The Hard Truth About Emma’s Childhood

Eric hasn’t tried to rewrite history. Emma was born in 1991 to Eric and his then-partner Kelly Cunningham. In his 2024 memoir Runaway Train: Or the Story of My Life So Far, Eric described himself brutally:

  • “A wash rag” emotionally
  • “A car wreck”
  • “Not to be depended on emotionally whatsoever”

He attributed his state to deep depression, substance abuse, and unresolved trauma from his own father, and admitted he abandoned Kelly when Emma was just seven months old.

Yet he also said he “fell madly in love with Emma the first minute I saw her” — a contradiction that captures why their relationship has always resisted a simple tabloid label.

What This Means For You: Lessons From Eric Roberts’ Candor

You don’t need a famous last name to find value in Eric’s recent interviews. A few takeaways that apply well beyond Hollywood:

  1. Own your worst quotes. Eric keeps calling his 2018 Vanity Fair remarks “asinine” — and that continued accountability is why the story is fading.
  2. Don’t let outsiders define your relationships. As Eric put it, outside eyes “don’t get any of it.” Most complicated family dynamics look different from the inside.
  3. Careers aren’t linear. A man who thought he was done at 27 is still working at 69, with a memoir and a new film, Clika, in his recent credits.
  4. Silence can be mistaken for estrangement. Eric and Julia rarely discuss each other publicly — not out of anger, but to avoid the “contest” framing.

A Common Misconception, Corrected

Many fans assumed Eric’s long stretches of not speaking about Julia or Emma meant something was wrong. In reality, it was a deliberate choice to protect their relationships from being flattened into a press narrative.

The Bottom Line: Eric Roberts Is Still Writing His Story

At 69, Eric Roberts is doing something most Hollywood veterans avoid — revisiting his regrets on the record. He’s clearing up a phantom feud, acknowledging real fatherhood failures, and crediting a 1983 Bob Fosse film for a career that almost never continued.

The surprising fact isn’t that Eric Roberts has a complicated family. It’s how willing he is, this late in the game, to talk about it plainly.

What do you think — does public honesty from celebrities change how you view their past controversies, or does the original story stick harder than the correction? The way you answer may say more about modern media than it does about the Roberts family.
