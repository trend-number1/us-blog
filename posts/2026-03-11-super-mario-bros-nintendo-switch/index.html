Super Mario Bros Nintendo Switch: The Ultimate Guide to Every Game Worth Playing

March 11, 2026 21:45 Trend Insights

Did you know that the Super Mario franchise has sold over 800 million copies worldwide, making it the best-selling video game franchise in history? And yet, many Nintendo Switch owners have played fewer than half of the Mario titles available on the platform. If you own a Switch, you are sitting on one of the richest libraries of Super Mario Bros content ever assembled on a single console.

Key Takeaways

  • The Nintendo Switch hosts more than a dozen Super Mario games spanning classic 2D platformers, 3D adventures, and spin-offs.
  • Super Mario Bros. Wonder introduced the most significant 2D Mario innovation in over a decade.
  • Nintendo Switch Online gives subscribers access to the original NES and SNES Mario titles at no extra cost.
  • Whether you prefer speedrunning, co-op play, or casual exploration, there is a Mario game on Switch built for your style.
  • Pricing varies widely, so knowing where to find deals can save you significant money.

The Evolution of Mario on Nintendo Switch

When the Nintendo Switch launched in March 2017, it arrived alongside Super Mario Odyssey, a game that went on to sell over 27 million copies and earn near-universal critical acclaim. That launch set the tone for what would become an extraordinary run of Mario content on the platform.

Since then, Nintendo has released new Mario titles, remastered beloved classics, and opened the vault of retro games through Nintendo Switch Online. The result is a console where you can play Mario’s NES debut from 1985 and his latest adventure without ever switching devices.

What makes the Switch library special is its range. You are not just getting one era of Mario. You are getting four decades of platforming history, all accessible from a handheld screen or your living room TV.

So where should you start? That depends on what kind of experience you are after.

Not all Mario games deliver the same experience. Here is how the major titles stack up across the categories that matter most to players.

Super Mario Odyssey remains the crown jewel of the Switch library. Its sandbox-style kingdoms, the hat-possession mechanic, and the sheer density of hidden moons make it one of the most replayable games on the platform. If you only buy one Mario game, this is the one.

Super Mario Bros. Wonder, released in October 2023, was the first entirely new 2D Mario game in over 11 years. The Wonder Flower mechanic, which transforms entire levels in unpredictable ways, was praised by critics and players alike as a breath of fresh air. It proved that 2D Mario still has the power to surprise.

For multiplayer households, Super Mario 3D World + Bowser’s Fury is hard to beat. The main campaign supports four players simultaneously, and the Bowser’s Fury add-on is a compact open-world experience that many consider a preview of where 3D Mario is heading next.

But here is something most guides will not tell you: the game that offers the most long-term value might be Super Mario Maker 2. With millions of user-created levels and a robust course builder, it is essentially an infinite Mario game. The community continues to produce inventive stages years after release.

Nintendo Switch Online: The Retro Mario Treasure Chest

One of the most overlooked ways to play Super Mario Bros on Nintendo Switch is through Nintendo Switch Online (NSO). A basic membership, priced at just $19.99 per year, grants access to a library of NES and SNES games that includes some of the greatest Mario titles ever made:

  • Super Mario Bros. (1985) – the game that started it all
  • Super Mario Bros. 2 (1988) – the quirky sequel with character-specific abilities
  • Super Mario Bros. 3 (1988) – widely regarded as one of the best platformers in history
  • Super Mario World (1990) – the SNES launch title that introduced Yoshi
  • Super Mario All-Stars (1993) – enhanced remakes of the NES trilogy

For an additional fee, the Expansion Pack tier ($49.99/year) adds N64 and Game Boy Advance titles, including Super Mario 64, the game that defined 3D platforming when it launched in 1996.

Here is a common misconception: many players assume these retro games feel outdated and are not worth revisiting. In reality, the tight controls and level design of Super Mario Bros. 3 and Super Mario World hold up remarkably well. Speedrunners still compete in these titles daily, and they regularly appear on “greatest games of all time” lists from outlets like IGN, GameSpot, and Edge Magazine.

The value proposition is staggering. For less than the cost of a single new game, you get access to the entire classic Mario catalog plus hundreds of other retro titles.

Tips and Strategies Most Players Overlook

Whether you are a newcomer or a veteran, there are strategies that can dramatically improve your experience with Mario games on the Switch.

Master the Momentum

In nearly every Mario game, momentum is king. Holding the run button before a jump gives you more distance and height. In Super Mario Bros. Wonder, this is especially important during the Wonder Flower segments, where split-second timing can mean the difference between reaching a secret area and missing it entirely.

Use Co-Op Strategically

Multiplayer Mario can be chaotic, but it is also a powerful tool. In New Super Mario Bros. U Deluxe, less experienced players can choose Nabbit, a character who cannot take damage from enemies. This lets skilled and casual players enjoy the same levels without frustration.

Do Not Ignore the Spin-Offs

Games like Super Mario RPG and Mario + Rabbids: Sparks of Hope are technically spin-offs, but they deliver some of the most memorable Mario experiences on the platform. Super Mario RPG, originally released in 1996 and remade for Switch in 2023, combines platforming with turn-based combat and a surprisingly emotional story. It sold over 3 million copies in its first month on Switch alone.

Check for Sales

Nintendo is known for rarely discounting first-party titles, but deals do appear. Nintendo eShop sales, Amazon Prime Day events, and Black Friday promotions have historically knocked 20-33% off Mario titles. Setting up price alerts on DekuDeals or checking the eShop weekly can save you real money.

What the Future Holds for Mario on Nintendo Hardware

With the Nintendo Switch 2 officially announced, the question on every Mario fan’s mind is: what comes next? Nintendo has confirmed backward compatibility, meaning your entire Switch Mario library will carry forward to the new hardware.

Rumors and leaks suggest a new 3D Mario game is in active development, potentially as a launch title or launch-window release for the Switch 2. If history is any guide, Nintendo will once again use Mario to showcase the capabilities of its new hardware, just as it did with Super Mario 64, Super Mario Galaxy, and Super Mario Odyssey.

Shigeru Miyamoto, the legendary creator of Mario, has indicated in interviews that the team is always exploring new gameplay ideas before settling on hardware features. This philosophy is why each new Mario game tends to feel genuinely fresh rather than iterative.

For now, the Nintendo Switch remains the single best platform to experience the breadth of Super Mario Bros history. No other console, past or present, offers this range of Mario content in one place.

Conclusion: Why Super Mario Bros on Nintendo Switch Still Matters

The Super Mario Bros Nintendo Switch library is not just a collection of games. It is a four-decade journey through one of the most important franchises in entertainment history. From the pixel-perfect precision of the 1985 original to the creative chaos of Super Mario Bros. Wonder, every era of Mario is playable on a single device.

Whether you are a parent introducing your kids to the Mushroom Kingdom for the first time, a speedrunner chasing world records, or a casual player looking for something joyful to unwind with, the Switch delivers.

The real question is not whether these games are worth playing. It is this: with so many incredible Mario experiences available right now, which one will you start with tonight?
