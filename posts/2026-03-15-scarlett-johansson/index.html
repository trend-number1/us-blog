Scarlett Johansson's Surprising 2026: From Anti-AI Crusade to The Exorcist Reboot

March 15, 2026 13:57 Trend Insights

The highest-grossing lead actress in box office history is fighting a war on multiple fronts — and winning on all of them.

Quick Summary: Scarlett Johansson is having a remarkably busy 2026. She’s spearheading a 700-person anti-AI campaign alongside Cate Blanchett, starring in Mike Flanagan’s new Exorcist film for Blumhouse, growing her skincare brand The Outset, and making her directorial debut with Eleanor the Great. At 41, she’s proving that Hollywood stardom extends far beyond the screen.

The Anti-AI Campaign That’s Shaking Silicon Valley

Scarlett Johansson has never been one to stay quiet on AI issues. After her public clash with OpenAI over a chatbot voice that sounded eerily similar to hers, she’s now escalating the fight to an industry-wide movement.

In January 2026, Johansson joined forces with Cate Blanchett and Joseph Gordon-Levitt to back a sweeping anti-AI campaign with a clear message: “Stealing our work is not innovation.” The initiative has attracted over 700 industry backers demanding stronger protections for creative professionals.

The campaign targets AI companies that train models on copyrighted work without permission or compensation. It’s a direct challenge to the tech industry’s stance that such training falls under fair use.

This isn’t Johansson’s first confrontation with AI overreach. The BBC previously covered her warning about “AI misuse” after a fake Kanye video circulated online. Her ongoing battle with OpenAI over voice cloning set a precedent that’s now fueling this broader movement.

What makes this campaign different from typical Hollywood activism? It has teeth. With 700 signatures from actors, writers, directors, and producers, it represents a unified creative industry pushing back against unchecked AI development.

A Bold New Take on The Exorcist

While fighting tech giants off-screen, Johansson is also taking on one of horror’s most iconic franchises.

She’ll star in Mike Flanagan’s new Exorcist film, produced by Blumhouse and Universal. Flanagan, known for his acclaimed Stephen King adaptations Gerald’s Game and Doctor Sleep, has promised a “fresh, bold” take on the franchise.

The cast assembled around Johansson is stacked with talent:

  • Laurence Fishburne — announced in February 2026
  • Chiwetel Ejiofor — confirmed the same week
  • John Leguizamo — joined in March 2026

This represents a major departure from the franchise’s recent struggles. Universal’s previous Exorcist sequel received mixed reviews, making Flanagan’s involvement a strategic reset. His track record with horror — from The Haunting of Hill House to Midnight Mass — suggests this could be the revival the franchise needs.

For Johansson, it’s also a genre pivot. While she’s no stranger to action through her decade with Marvel’s Black Widow, a horror lead represents new territory for the actress.

From Box Office Queen to Beauty Mogul

Johansson’s business ventures are proving just as formidable as her acting career. The Outset, her skincare brand, continues to expand with new product launches.

The brand recently dropped a spot-lightening serum described as “blue light glasses for your skin,” targeting the damage caused by prolonged screen exposure. A new Lip Oasis Resort Collection featuring three new colors has also launched.

What’s notable about The Outset is its positioning. Rather than competing in the crowded celebrity beauty space with flashy marketing, the brand has focused on dermatologist-backed formulations. Reviews highlight its effectiveness for eczema and facial dry patches — conditions that more glamorous brands often overlook.

Speaking of commercials, Johansson teamed up with Jon Hamm and Bowen Yang for a Ritz crackers Super Bowl spot in February. It’s a reminder that even at the top of Hollywood, Super Bowl ads remain irresistible.

The Box Office Record That Was Broken

There’s a fascinating subplot to Johansson’s 2026 story. She previously held the record as the highest-grossing lead actor at the global box office, with a career lead-project gross of $14.8 billion that surpassed fellow Marvel alumni Robert Downey Jr. and Samuel L. Jackson.

However, that record was recently surpassed by Zoe Saldaña, thanks to the latest Avatar film. Johansson’s husband, Saturday Night Live anchor Colin Jost, hilariously addressed this on air during a January 2026 episode, turning it into comedy gold.

Despite losing the top spot, Johansson’s career earnings remain staggering. Forbes’ 2025 list of highest-paid actors revealed that the top 20 collectively earned an estimated $590 million — though that figure was down about 20% from $730 million in 2024, reflecting broader industry anxiety around AI and declining theatrical ticket sales.

What This Means for Hollywood’s Future

Scarlett Johansson’s 2026 trajectory tells a larger story about where Hollywood is heading. She’s simultaneously embracing traditional filmmaking with The Exorcist, challenging technological disruption through the anti-AI campaign, and building a business empire beyond acting.

At 41, she’s navigating the industry’s most pressing tensions: the promise and threat of AI, the shift from theatrical to streaming, and the evolution of celebrity into entrepreneurship.

Her directorial debut Eleanor the Great, starring June Squibb and Erin Kellyman, premiered at the 2025 Toronto International Film Festival before its theatrical release. It signals yet another dimension to a career that refuses to be defined by a single lane.

The question worth asking: in an era where AI can generate performances, replicate voices, and create digital likenesses, will Johansson’s anti-AI crusade reshape how Hollywood protects its talent? Or will technology inevitably outpace regulation? Either way, few voices in the industry carry as much weight as hers.
