Patrick Brammall: The Surprising Aussie Stealing Prada 2

Imagine shooting your first Hollywood scene surrounded by roughly 300 paparazzi and onlookers, then going home to find friends texting you pictures of the chaos. That was Patrick Brammall’s welcome to “The Devil Wears Prada 2,” and the 50-year-old Australian actor is now one of the most talked-about new names in the sequel.

Quick Summary

  • Who: Patrick Brammall, 50, Australian AACTA and Logie-winning actor
  • Role: Peter, Anne Hathaway’s new love interest in “The Devil Wears Prada 2”
  • Release: In theaters May 1, 2026
  • Why it matters: It’s his first big-screen role since 2018’s “Overlord,” placing him opposite Meryl Streep, Emily Blunt, and Stanley Tucci.

Who Is Patrick Brammall?

If the name Patrick Brammall sounds new, you’re not alone — but longtime TV fans likely already know him. He’s best known as the co-creator, co-writer, and co-star of the hit romantic comedy “Colin From Accounts,” which he makes with his wife, Harriet Dyer. The show streams on Paramount+ and has earned him a loyal following on both sides of the Pacific.

Over the past eight years, Brammall has built his career primarily on the small screen, with credits that include “No Activity,” “Bluey,” and “Evil.” He is also set to star in “The Dispatcher,” an upcoming limited series on Apple TV+, which means 2026 could be the year Brammall finally becomes a household name in the U.S.

He has won both AACTA and Logie awards, Australia’s top screen honors — the equivalent of grabbing Emmys and Golden Globes on home turf. Not bad for an actor Americans are only now discovering.

Transition: So how does an Australian TV star land the most buzzed-about rom-com role of 2026?

The Devil Wears Prada 2 Role That Changed Everything

In the sequel, Anne Hathaway reprises her role as Andy Sachs, now working as the features editor at Runway Magazine. The first film paired Andy with Nate (played by Adrian Grenier of “Entourage”), but Grenier is not returning. Instead, Andy has a new love interest: Peter, played by Brammall.

That makes his casting the emotional anchor of Andy’s storyline. In a franchise stacked with returning legends — Meryl Streep, Emily Blunt, Stanley Tucci — Brammall is the new face audiences will watch Andy fall for.

Filming made headlines fast. On August 5, 2025, paparazzi photographed Brammall and Hathaway shooting scenes in Brooklyn, New York. The images went viral, and Brammall admitted he only understood the scale when friends started forwarding the pictures to him.

“That was a bit mental,” he told USA TODAY at the film’s world premiere. “I think I might be in an iconic movie.”

Hathaway, he says, made a point of making him feel comfortable on set, calling her simply “the best.”

Transition: The buzz isn’t just anecdotal — the numbers are historic.

Why This Sequel Is Already a Blockbuster

“The Devil Wears Prada 2” is not sneaking into theaters. The film’s second trailer pulled 222 million views in 24 hours, which 20th Century Studios called its most-viewed trailer in history. That’s the kind of number that rewrites marketing playbooks.

Here’s how the sequel’s newcomer cast stacks up against the returning talent:

Reportedly, some cameos were cut from the final film, including one from “Euphoria” star Sydney Sweeney.

The plot, per reporting, follows Miranda Priestly (Streep) navigating the decline of print journalism and trying to find her footing in a dying industry — with Emily Blunt’s character emerging as her new rival. It’s a premise that swaps the original’s fish-out-of-water comedy for something sharper and more timely.

Transition: Brammall’s press run shows just how carefully the studio is rolling him out.

Inside Brammall’s Press Tour

Unlike the returning stars, Brammall hasn’t been front and center in the promotional push — so the studio is giving him dedicated visibility in the final stretch before opening weekend.

Key stops on his promo tour include:

  1. “GMA3” appearance on ABC, Tuesday, April 21, 2026, at 1:00 PM
  2. World premiere in New York, where he spoke with USA TODAY
  3. Yahoo video interview discussing the role and the sequel
  4. Sydney Gala premiere at the State Theatre on Tuesday, April 28, where he’ll walk the red carpet as the local lead

The Sydney premiere is especially notable. While the sequel is dominated by Hollywood A-listers, Brammall is the Australian face of the film — and he’s being positioned that way domestically. For his home country, his casting is a genuine breakthrough moment.

Asked by USA TODAY what it was like to play Hathaway’s love interest, Brammall laughed off the gossip:

“I tell them, ‘None of your business. What happens on set stays on set.’”

Transition: So what does all of this mean if you’re just a casual viewer?

What This Means For You

If you only know Brammall from “Colin From Accounts” — or not at all — “The Devil Wears Prada 2” is about to change that. Here’s how to think about his rise:

  • For fans of the original Prada: Expect a very different romantic arc. Nate is gone; Peter is the new emotional lens for Andy.
  • For fans of smart TV comedy: Use this moment to catch up on “Colin From Accounts” on Paramount+ before Brammall becomes harder to book.
  • For industry watchers: His trajectory — long TV buildup, award wins, then a franchise break — is the modern blueprint for “overnight” stardom.
  • For Australian viewers: He’s the local lead in one of the year’s biggest global releases. That’s a rare crossover.

Common misconception: Many assume Brammall is a newcomer plucked from obscurity. In reality, he’s a decades-deep career actor who has quietly built an award-winning résumé on television, including a starring role in a series he co-created with his spouse. “The Devil Wears Prada 2” is less a debut and more a long-earned graduation.

Another misconception: that replacing Nate with a new love interest is a minor tweak. It isn’t — it signals that the sequel is recalibrating the franchise’s emotional geometry, and Brammall is at the center of that decision.

The Bottom Line

Patrick Brammall has done something rare: he’s walking into the sequel of a beloved 2006 hit as the new romantic lead, opposite Anne Hathaway, in a film whose trailer already broke a studio record. With “The Devil Wears Prada 2” opening May 1 and “The Dispatcher” on Apple TV+ still to come, his next twelve months could reshape how global audiences think about Australian leading men.

So here’s a question worth sitting with: when was the last time a “new” face in a sequel actually made you care about the franchise again? If Brammall pulls it off, he won’t just be Andy’s new love interest — he’ll be the reason Prada 2 works.
