Bill Murray's Son Luke Murray Hired as Boston College Basketball Coach

March 28, 2026 12:03 Trend Insights

Bill Murray has long been one of the most recognizable faces in college basketball arenas — not as a player or coach, but as the most famous dad in the stands. Now, his son Luke Murray is stepping into the spotlight on his own terms, hired as the new head coach of Boston College men’s basketball.

Key Takeaways

  • Luke Murray, son of actor Bill Murray, has been hired as Boston College’s 14th head basketball coach
  • He replaces Earl Grant, who went 72-92 overall and 30-67 in ACC play over five seasons
  • Luke Murray helped build UConn’s back-to-back national championship teams in 2023 and 2024
  • Boston College hasn’t made the NCAA Tournament since 2009 — a 17-year drought
  • Bill Murray was spotted at UConn’s Sweet 16 game against Michigan State on March 28

Luke Murray Takes the Helm at Boston College

Boston College officially hired UConn assistant Luke Murray on Thursday as the new head coach of its struggling men’s basketball program. The Eagles have gone 17 years without an NCAA Tournament appearance, making this one of the most challenging rebuilding jobs in the ACC.

Murray replaces Earl Grant, who was fired earlier this month after compiling a 72-92 overall record and a dismal 30-67 mark in Atlantic Coast Conference games across five seasons. The Eagles managed a losing record in four of Grant’s five years at the helm.

“In Luke Murray, we have found a leader who does not just understand the modern landscape of college basketball, he has helped define it,” Boston College athletic director Blake James said. “His role in building a national championship caliber program, his sophisticated offensive vision, and his relentless pursuit of excellence make him the perfect fit.”

This will be Luke Murray’s first head coaching position — a major leap from assistant roles, but one backed by an impressive resume.

A Resume Built on Championship Pedigree

Luke Murray isn’t riding his father’s fame to the top. He has spent nearly two decades grinding through the coaching ranks at every level of college basketball.

Here’s a look at his coaching journey:

  • 2007 — Started at Quinnipiac as director of basketball operations
  • Held assistant positions at Post University and Arizona (graduate assistant)
  • Spent three years each as an assistant at Louisville and Xavier
  • Also coached at Towson and Wagner
  • Joined UConn under Dan Hurley, where he spent five seasons

His time at UConn is what truly elevated his profile. Murray was one of the key assistant coaches on Dan Hurley’s staff that delivered something exceedingly rare in modern college basketball — back-to-back national championships in 2023 and 2024. He established himself as what insiders call an “elite offensive tactician,” a reputation that made him a prime target for programs looking to rebuild.

A 2007 graduate of Fairfield, Murray becomes the 14th head coach in Boston College program history. The timing is notable — he was hired while UConn is still alive in this year’s NCAA Tournament, with the Huskies playing Michigan State in the Sweet 16.

Bill Murray: The Most Famous Basketball Dad in America

If you’ve watched UConn basketball over the past five years, you’ve almost certainly spotted Bill Murray in the crowd. The beloved actor, now 74 years old, has been a fixture at Huskies games, cheering on his son’s team through two championship runs.

That tradition continued this week. Bill Murray was in attendance at UConn’s Sweet 16 game against Michigan State on March 28, a bittersweet moment given that his son had just been hired away from the program.

With Luke heading to Chestnut Hill, don’t be surprised to see Bill Murray trading his UConn gear for Boston College maroon and gold. As one local outlet noted, “Bill Murray could be spotted around Boston soon” — and Eagles fans are already buzzing about the star power his presence could bring to a program desperate for attention.

The Boston College Challenge: Reviving a Dormant Program

Make no mistake — Luke Murray is walking into one of the toughest jobs in Power Five basketball. Boston College has been buried in the supersized ACC for over a decade.

The numbers tell a grim story:

  • Last NCAA Tournament appearance: 2008-09 season (lost in the first round)
  • Best conference finish since the ACC expanded to 15 teams in 2013-14: 10th place (2022-23)
  • Earl Grant’s ACC record: 30-67 (.309 winning percentage)

The Eagles need more than a coaching change — they need a cultural transformation. Murray’s experience at UConn, where he watched Dan Hurley turn a program into a dynasty, could provide the blueprint. His offensive expertise and recruiting connections built over nearly 20 years in the coaching pipeline will be tested immediately.

The ACC is a brutal conference, and Boston College will need to compete against blue-blood programs with far more resources and history. But Murray’s championship pedigree gives Eagles fans something they haven’t had in years: genuine hope.

Bill Murray’s Complicated Year in the Spotlight

While Luke Murray’s hiring is a feel-good story, Bill Murray has also been in the news for more difficult reasons. The actor has continued to address the 2022 misconduct allegations that led to the cancellation of the film “Being Mortal,” directed by Aziz Ansari.

According to reports, a female production assistant accused Murray of straddling her on a bed and kissing her through their COVID masks during production. Searchlight Pictures launched an internal investigation, determined the allegation had merit, and indefinitely suspended production — even though the film was nearly finished shooting.

Murray and the unnamed staffer reached a settlement of just over $100,000 in October 2022. In a recent interview with The New York Times, Murray said the incident still weighs on him. “I don’t go too many days or weeks without thinking of what happened in ‘Being Mortal,’” he said. “I tried to make peace. I ended up being, to my mind, barbecued.”

Ansari, speaking to The Hollywood Reporter, said Murray was “devastated” by the accusations and the film’s cancellation. “He couldn’t believe it happened. I think this movie meant a lot to him,” Ansari said, adding that he still hopes to one day complete the project with Murray attached.

Murray has also spoken about a 19th-century painting at the Art Institute of Chicago that he says “saved his life,” offering a glimpse into the emotional toll the past few years have taken on the legendary comedian.

What This Means for College Basketball Fans

Luke Murray’s hiring at Boston College is part of a broader trend in college basketball: programs betting on proven assistants from championship-caliber staffs rather than recycling the same carousel of retreaded head coaches.

For Bill Murray fans, it means one of Hollywood’s most unpredictable personalities will likely become a regular presence at ACC games — bringing cameras, attention, and a touch of celebrity magic to a program that desperately needs all three.

The real question is whether Luke Murray can translate his UConn success into results at a program with far fewer built-in advantages. Can he recruit against Duke, North Carolina, and the rest of the ACC’s elite? Can he end a 17-year NCAA Tournament drought?

One thing is certain: with a Murray on the sideline and another Murray in the stands, Boston College basketball just became appointment television.
