Hawks vs Mavericks: Atlanta's Historic 10-Game Win Streak Faces Dallas Test

March 19, 2026 09:07 Trend Insights

Hawks vs Mavericks is one of the most lopsided matchups on the NBA schedule this week, and the numbers tell a striking story. Atlanta has not lost a game in nearly three weeks, while Dallas cannot seem to find a win. When the Hawks visit American Airlines Center on Wednesday, March 18 at 8:30 PM EDT, they will be looking to extend a streak the franchise has not seen in over a decade.

Key Takeaways:

  • The Hawks are riding a 10-game winning streak, their longest since the 2014-15 season
  • Atlanta has won 9 of those 10 games by double digits
  • Dallas has dropped 8 of its last 10 games and sits at 23-46
  • Hawks are 8.5-point favorites with a -330 moneyline
  • Nickeil Alexander-Walker is averaging 20.3 PPG during the recent stretch

A Dominant Streak Puts Atlanta in Rare Company

The Atlanta Hawks (37-31, 8th in the Eastern Conference) are playing the best basketball of their season at the perfect time. Their 10-game winning streak is the franchise’s longest since the memorable 2014-15 campaign, and the manner of these victories is what stands out most.

Nine of those ten wins have come by double-digit margins. This is not a team grinding out close games. Atlanta is dismantling opponents with a combination of elite ball movement, efficient shooting, and opportunistic defense.

During the streak, the Hawks rank 7th in the league in scoring, 7th in three-point percentage, and an impressive 1st in assists. Defensively, they sit 5th in steals, creating turnovers and turning them into fast-break opportunities.

Their most recent outing was a convincing 124-112 victory over the Orlando Magic, a game in which Nickeil Alexander-Walker erupted for a career-caliber 41 points.

Cooper Flagg Leads a Rebuilding Dallas

On the other side, the Dallas Mavericks (23-46, 13th in the Western Conference) are in full rebuild mode, and the results reflect it. Dallas has gone 2-8 in its last 10 games and is just 14-20 at home this season.

The bright spot is rookie sensation Cooper Flagg, who is averaging 20.2 points, 6.6 rebounds, and 4.5 assists per game. Flagg has been a dominant force in the paint, averaging 11.2 points per game in that area alone, helping Dallas rank 2nd in the Western Conference with 53.2 points in the paint.

However, individual brilliance has not translated to team success. The Mavericks are allowing 118.2 points per game, and their most recent loss was a 129-111 blowout at the hands of the New Orleans Pelicans on Monday. Injury concerns add to the struggles, with Jonathan Kuminga listed on the injury report due to a lingering knee issue and Klay Thompson’s status uncertain.

Head-to-Head Breakdown

The season series already favors Atlanta, which won the first meeting 124-112 on March 10. Nickeil Alexander-Walker scored 29 points in that contest, establishing himself as the primary offensive weapon against Dallas.

The matchup between Onyeka Okongwu (15.8 PPG, 7.8 RPG) and Flagg in the paint could be the most compelling individual battle of the night. Okongwu’s physicality and rebounding will be tested against Flagg’s athleticism and scoring instincts.

Key Players to Watch

Several players will shape the outcome of this contest:

  • Nickeil Alexander-Walker (ATL) — Averaging 20.3 PPG and 2.6 RPG over the recent stretch. Scored 29 in the first matchup and 41 in the most recent game against Orlando. He is the engine driving Atlanta’s offense.
  • Cooper Flagg (DAL) — The rookie averaging 20.2 PPG is Dallas’s best hope for keeping this competitive. His paint dominance will be crucial against Atlanta’s interior defense.
  • Onyeka Okongwu (ATL) — Contributing 15.8 PPG and 7.8 RPG, Okongwu provides the Hawks with a reliable second scoring option and dominant rebounding presence.
  • Naji Marshall (DAL) — Averaging 12 PPG and 3.3 APG over the last 10 games, Marshall has been one of the few consistent contributors for Dallas during their slide.

The potential absence of Kuminga and Thompson could leave Dallas even thinner, forcing Flagg and Marshall to shoulder an even heavier workload.

Betting Lines and Prediction

The oddsmakers see this one clearly. Atlanta enters as 8.5-point favorites with a -330 moneyline, while Dallas sits at +265. Those numbers reflect the enormous gap between these two teams right now.

A few betting trends worth noting:

  • The Hawks are 9-1 ATS during the winning streak
  • Atlanta is 16-9 ATS against non-conference opponents, the best mark in the league
  • The Over/Under has leaned toward the Under during the streak, with the Hawks going 4-6 to the Over
  • Dallas is 4-6 ATS in the last 10 games

The Hawks’ defensive metrics during the streak are interesting. They rank 21st in points allowed, which is far from elite, but their 5th-ranked steals rate compensates by generating extra possessions. Combined with their league-leading assist numbers, Atlanta creates quality shots while forcing opponents into rushed ones.

Atlanta is 6-7 in one-possession games this season, which suggests some vulnerability in tight contests. But given that 9 of their last 10 wins came by double digits, the Hawks appear unlikely to let this game come down to the wire.

Everything points toward another comfortable Atlanta victory. The Hawks have the momentum, the superior roster health, the favorable season-series history, and a level of confidence that only a 10-game winning streak can provide. Dallas will lean on Flagg’s brilliance, but one player cannot overcome the systematic advantages Atlanta brings to the floor right now.

The real question is not whether the Hawks will win, but whether they can push this streak to 11 and keep building toward a stronger playoff seed as the regular season winds down. For Dallas, the focus remains on developing Flagg and building the foundation for a brighter future.
