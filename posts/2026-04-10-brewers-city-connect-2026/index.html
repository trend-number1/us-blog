Brewers City Connect 2026: The 'Wisco Way' Uniforms Go Beyond Milwaukee

April 10, 2026 00:03 Trend Insights

The Milwaukee Brewers just dropped one of the most ambitious City Connect uniforms in MLB history — and it’s not even about Milwaukee this time. Unveiled on April 9, 2026, the new “Wisco Way” City Connect jersey embraces the entire state of Wisconsin, from its Great Lakes shorelines to its Northwoods sunsets.

Key Takeaways:

  • The Brewers’ 2026 City Connect features a scripted “Wisco” wordmark, replacing the previous “Brew Crew” design
  • Design elements honor Wisconsin’s lakes, forests, supper clubs, and brewing heritage
  • The uniforms debut on the field April 10 vs. the Washington Nationals
  • Eight MLB teams revealed new City Connect 2.0 uniforms as part of Nike’s three-year rotation cycle
  • Jerseys and merchandise are available now through MLB, Nike, and Fanatics

Brewers City Connect Design: Every Detail Tells a Wisconsin Story

Where the 2022 City Connect celebrated Milwaukee specifically — with a hidden “414” area code, a grill patch, and beer foam-inspired striping — this second installment goes statewide.

The base blue was inspired by Wisconsin’s endless shorelines, lakes, and rivers. An accent cream represents the sandy shores of inland lakes, Door County beaches, and the state’s sandstone bluffs. And the sunset gradient woven through the typography captures those golden Northwoods summer evenings that Wisconsinites know by heart.

The scripted “Wisco” across the chest is the centerpiece. The typography blends influences from three distinctly Wisconsin sources:

  • Early 20th-century American Association Milwaukee Brewers script lettering
  • Classic Wisconsin supper club signage
  • Vintage brewery labels from the state’s legendary beer heritage

A reimagined Barrelman logo sits inside the outline of the state on the sleeve patch, while smaller details reference fishing, lake life, barley fields, and Cream City brick. Wisconsin’s state motto, “Forward,” is stitched inside the collar.

“Wisconsin is a special place — from its lakes and rivers to its farms, forests and festivals,” said Brewers President of Business Operations Rich Schlesinger. “This uniform is meaningful because it reflects the entire state we represent.”

So what happened to the beloved powder blue? It lives on. The popular color has already been carried over into the club’s new 2026 road uniform, ensuring fans who loved that look won’t lose it entirely.

How the 2026 City Connect Compares to the 2022 Original

The two Brewers City Connect jerseys take remarkably different approaches while sharing the same DNA. Here’s how they stack up:

Brewers COO Marti Wronski told SportsLogos.Net that the first City Connect had already done its job honoring Milwaukee and the “Brew Crew” identity. Going through the process a second time, the challenge was to create something different enough that it wouldn’t feel like a variation of the first.

That’s a tricky balance. Expand the scope without losing specificity. The “Wisco” concept threads that needle — it’s broader geographically but arguably more personal, tapping into the lake houses, supper clubs, and sunsets that define Wisconsin summers for millions of fans across all 72 counties.

MLB’s City Connect 2.0 Wave: Eight Teams, Eight Stories

The Brewers weren’t alone in Thursday’s reveal. Eight MLB clubs unveiled second-generation City Connect uniforms as part of the program’s three-year rotation cycle, first launched by Nike in 2021.

The full 2026 City Connect class includes:

  1. Atlanta Braves — 1980s powder blue throwback with modernized red piping and updated “Atlanta” script
  2. Baltimore Orioles — New design details pending
  3. Cincinnati Reds — New design details pending
  4. Kansas City Royals — New design details pending
  5. Milwaukee Brewers — Statewide “Wisco” theme with sunset gradient
  6. Pittsburgh Pirates — New design details pending
  7. San Diego Padres — New design details pending
  8. Texas Rangers — New design details pending

Each club partnered with Nike and Fanatics, MLB’s uniform manufacturer, to build on its previous City Connect styling and explore new aspects of their city, region, and fan base. These new uniforms will remain part of each club’s on-field rotation for multiple seasons.

MLB described the new wave as reflective of “the energy and pride of each club’s community, offering bold and expressive interpretations that celebrate both team history and what’s ahead for the game.”

Fan Reactions and the Leak That Started It All

The Brewers’ design didn’t arrive without controversy. Photos of the jersey leaked on social media days before the official reveal, giving fans an early — and incomplete — preview.

Social media user Steve Freed posted images showing only the front and back, revealing the “Great Lakes blue” colorway, “Wisco” chest script, and “Forward” collar text. Without seeing the sleeve details, gradient effects, and full patch work, initial reactions were mixed.

Many fans noted the design leans more toward Wisconsin as a whole rather than Milwaukee, which was a deliberate shift. The “Great Lakes blue” also drew comparisons to the Milwaukee Bucks’ City Edition uniforms, which have featured similar shades.

But seeing the complete uniform in its full reveal context — with the sunset gradient typography, the Barrelman-in-state-outline patch, and the fishing and lake life references — filled in the story that static leaked photos couldn’t tell. As with most City Connect releases, the on-field debut often changes opinions.

The Brewers will wear the “Wisco Way” jerseys for the first time on Friday, April 10, against the Washington Nationals at American Family Field.

Where to Buy the Brewers City Connect 2026 Jersey

Merchandise is available immediately through multiple retailers:

  • MLBShop.com — Full range of jerseys, caps, and accessories
  • Nike.com — Authentic and replica jerseys
  • Fanatics — Jerseys, hats, t-shirts, and more
  • American Family Field team store — In-person availability on game days

Expect the standard City Connect pricing: authentic jerseys in the $150–$300+ range depending on customization, with replica options and t-shirts at lower price points. Given the popularity of the original powder blue, demand for the “Wisco” gear could move fast.

What This Means for You

If you’re a Brewers fan, this jersey is designed to speak to your Wisconsin identity — not just your Milwaukee fandom. It’s a bet by the organization that the team’s reach extends across every county in the state, from the FoxValley to the Driftless Area.

For MLB uniform collectors and City Connect enthusiasts, this second wave signals that teams are getting bolder and more narrative-driven with their designs. The three-year rotation cycle means every City Connect now has a sequel to live up to — or surpass.

And if you loved the powder blue? It’s not gone. It just moved to road games.

Will the “Wisco Way” become as beloved as the original “Brew Crew” City Connect? That question gets answered on the field starting tomorrow — and in the stands, where Wisconsin fans will decide with their wallets and their cheers.
