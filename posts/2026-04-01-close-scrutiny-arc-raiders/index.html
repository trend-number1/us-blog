Close Scrutiny Arc Raiders Guide: Surviving the Brutal New Endgame Map Condition

April 1, 2026 18:03 Trend Insights

What if the safest loot runs you’ve memorized suddenly became the most dangerous zones on the map? That’s exactly what Embark Studios just did with the Flashpoint update for Arc Raiders, dropping the new Close Scrutiny map condition that fundamentally rewrites how you approach every raid.

Key Takeaways

  • Close Scrutiny is Arc Raiders’ first ARC Operation, a new endgame map condition launched March 31, 2026 (Patch 1.22.0).
  • The Assessor, a massive stationary probe, lands on the map carrying high-value loot guarded by heavy ARC patrols.
  • A new flying enemy, the Vaporizer, uses devastating laser attacks and travels in groups.
  • Shredders now roam all exterior maps, not just Stella Montis.
  • Overall map loot is reduced, but rewards near the Assessor are significantly higher.
  • Available on Blue Gate, Buried City, Spaceport, and Dam Battlegrounds — Stella Montis is excluded.

Close Scrutiny Explained: Arc Raiders’ First ARC Operation

The Flashpoint update (Patch 1.22.0), which went live on March 31, 2026 across PC, PlayStation 5, and Xbox Series X/S, introduces ARC Operations as an entirely new category of major map condition. Close Scrutiny is the first of its kind, and Embark Studios has explicitly labeled it endgame difficulty.

Unlike the previous Hurricane map condition from the Shrouded Sky update, Close Scrutiny isn’t weather-based. Instead, it revolves around a hostile military occupation of the map by ARC forces. When this condition is active, the entire dynamic of a raid shifts:

  • Locked doors are disabled — every room is accessible.
  • Overall loot value across the map decreases — your usual scavenging spots run dry.
  • High-value resources concentrate around the Assessor — risk and reward are tightly coupled.
  • ARC patrol presence spikes dramatically — expect far more enemies than a standard raid.

The design philosophy is clear: Embark stripped away the passive loot experience and replaced it with high-value targets that actively try to kill you. If your loadout is “aspirational rather than functional,” as one guide puts it, you might want a different lobby.

So what exactly is waiting for you in those danger zones? Let’s break down the two new threats.

The Assessor: A Three-Legged Loot Fortress

The Assessor is the centerpiece of Close Scrutiny. It’s a massive, three-legged ARC probe that makes a controlled descent from the sky and physically lands on the map. You’ll hear a distinct sound when it touches down, and multiple Assessors can spawn across different regions during a single raid.

Here’s what makes the Assessor unique compared to other ARC threats:

FeatureAssessorStandard ARC Units
MovementCompletely stationaryRoaming patrols

The Assessor doesn’t chase you. It doesn’t need to. It holds ground, surrounds itself with a dense perimeter of ARC patrols, and dares you to push in. The core tension of every Close Scrutiny raid boils down to one decision: play it safe on the periphery with diminished loot, or push into the Assessor’s kill zone for the real rewards.

Embark’s narrative framing adds flavor — the in-game character Celeste wants intelligence on what the Assessor is doing, while Raiders just want whatever it’s holding. Two birds, one stone. But getting there alive is the challenge.

The Vaporizer and Expanded Shredders: New Threats Everywhere

If the Assessor is the anchor, the Vaporizer is the chaos agent. This new flying ARC unit patrols the areas around ARC Operations and represents a noticeably different threat from existing aerial enemies like the Wasp, Hornet, or Firefly.

What makes the Vaporizer dangerous:

  • Laser-based attacks — unlike the Firefly’s fire, the Vaporizer uses precision lasers.
  • Unpredictable movement patterns — it doesn’t follow the predictable patrol routes of other drones.
  • Group behavior — Vaporizers travel in packs, meaning you’re rarely fighting just one.
  • Concentrated around ARC Operations — they specifically guard the Assessor zones.

The February Shrouded Sky update introduced the Firefly and the explosive Comet. The Flashpoint update adds only one new ARC type, but the Vaporizer’s group tactics and laser attacks make it a serious threat multiplier, especially when combined with ground patrols near the Assessor.

Then there are the Shredders. Previously confined to the indoor corridors of Stella Montis — where you could plan around their positions — Shredders now roam freely across Blue Gate, Buried City, Spaceport, and Dam Battlegrounds during Close Scrutiny. The Shredder was recently crowned the deadliest ARC in Arc Raiders, and now it’s no longer contained. Players who mastered indoor Shredder avoidance need to relearn how to handle them in open environments.

This layering of threats is what gives Close Scrutiny its endgame reputation. You’re not just fighting one new enemy — you’re dealing with Assessor patrols, Vaporizer laser squads, and roaming Shredders simultaneously.

New Weapons and Gear for the Fight

Embark didn’t send Raiders into this meat grinder empty-handed. The Flashpoint update ships with two new weapons designed for the kinds of engagements Close Scrutiny creates:

  1. The Canto — A medium-ammo SMG built for close-range combat against both ARC machines and other Raiders. In the tight patrol zones around Assessors, having a reliable close-quarters option matters.
  2. Energy Shotgun — Features a unique variable-focus mechanic, giving it versatility that standard shotguns lack.

A new deployable also ships with the update, though details on its specific function are still emerging from player testing.

The weapon choices signal Embark’s intent: Close Scrutiny fights happen at close to medium range. You’re pushing into fortified positions, not sniping from a hillside. Build your loadout accordingly.

Survival Tips: What This Means for You

Whether you’re a veteran Raider or still learning the ropes, Close Scrutiny demands a shift in strategy. Here’s actionable advice based on the update’s mechanics:

Before you queue:

  • Bring your best gear. This is endgame content. Budget loadouts will get you killed.
  • Prioritize close-range weapons. The Canto SMG and shotguns shine in Assessor zones.
  • Squad up. Solo raids during Close Scrutiny are possible but punishing. Coordinated teams can divide patrol attention.

During the raid:

  • Listen for the Assessor landing sound. It signals where high-value loot is concentrating.
  • Watch the skies. Vaporizers patrol in groups near the Assessor — clear them before pushing in.
  • Don’t ignore Shredders. They’re no longer predictable indoor threats. Keep your head on a swivel in every exterior area.
  • Use unlocked doors strategically. With all doors open, you have more escape routes than usual.

A common misconception: Some players assume Close Scrutiny means less total loot. The reality is more nuanced — overall map loot value decreases, but the concentrated rewards near the Assessor can exceed what you’d normally extract from a full standard run. The loot moved; it didn’t disappear.

The Bigger Picture: Where Arc Raiders Is Heading

The Flashpoint update represents Embark Studios’ most significant content drop of March 2026, and the introduction of ARC Operations as a map condition category — not just a one-off event — suggests Close Scrutiny is a template for future endgame content.

The update also includes a community antenna project and the Wasp Hunter cosmetic bundle, signaling Embark’s investment in both gameplay depth and community engagement.

Arc Raiders continues to evolve its extraction-shooter formula by layering risk-reward decisions into every aspect of the raid loop. Close Scrutiny doesn’t just add enemies — it restructures the entire loot economy of a map and forces players to make strategic choices they didn’t have to make before.

The question every Raider needs to answer: is the Assessor’s loot worth the Vaporizer’s laser? Based on early community reactions, the answer is a resounding — and often fatal — yes.
