Season Finale Schedule 2026: Every Major Show’s Ending Date You Need to Know

Spring means one thing for TV fans: finale season is here. With more than 60 shows across the major broadcast networks preparing to sign off in the coming weeks, keeping track of when your favorites wrap up has never been more critical.

Key Takeaways

  • Over 60 broadcast shows will air season finales between April and May 2026
  • ABC finales run from April 7 (High Potential) through May 15 (Celebrity Jeopardy!)
  • CBS kicks off April 23 with Matlock and wraps May 27 with Hollywood Squares
  • Fox started early with Fear Factor: House of Fear in late March
  • Several series finales are confirmed, including Watson and The Neighborhood on CBS
  • Dark Winds Season 4 just delivered a shocking finale on AMC

Whether you’re planning your DVR, coordinating watch parties, or just dreading the long summer hiatus, here’s your complete guide to every confirmed 2026 season finale date across ABC, CBS, Fox, and NBC — plus a look at the cable finale everyone’s talking about.

ABC’s Season Finale Lineup: Scrubs, Grey’s, and The Rookie Close the Book

ABC has officially locked in its finale dates for the 2025–2026 television season, and the schedule is stacked. The network’s farewell episodes span beloved dramas, rebooted comedies, and unscripted hits.

The action starts early. High Potential’s Season 2 finale arrives on April 7, making it one of the first major finales of the spring. Comedy fans won’t have to wait much longer — Scrubs closes its rebooted Season 1 on April 15, a milestone for the long-awaited revival.

The rest of April belongs to comedies and reality:

  • April 21 — Abbott Elementary (Season 5), The Greatest Average American (Season 1), Shark Tank (Season 17)
  • April 28 — RJ Decker (Season 1)

Then comes the drama-heavy first week of May:

  • May 4 — The Rookie (Season 8)
  • May 5 — Will Trent (Season 4)
  • May 7 — Grey’s Anatomy (Season 22), 9-1-1 (Season 9), 9-1-1: Nashville (Season 1)

Unscripted programming rounds out the calendar, with American Idol wrapping Season 24 on May 11 and Celebrity Jeopardy! closing Season 4 on May 15. All finale episodes will stream on Hulu the following day.

The biggest night on ABC’s schedule? May 7 is a triple-header, with Grey’s Anatomy, 9-1-1, and its Nashville spinoff all airing finales on the same Thursday. That’s appointment television at its most intense.

So what’s happening over on CBS? The answer: some major goodbyes.

CBS Season Finales: Matlock, NCIS, and Permanent Goodbyes

CBS revealed its finale schedule in March, and it includes both exciting cliffhanger setups and genuine series enders for shows that won’t be returning.

The CBS finale season opens on Thursday, April 23 with the Season 2 finale of Matlock, starring Kathy Bates. From there, the network rolls through its primetime lineup over five weeks.

The most significant takeaway? Watson and DMV have both been canceled, ranking as the least-watched series across CBS’s primetime lineup. The Neighborhood is also ending its eight-season run. All other CBS series are confirmed to return for the 2026–2027 season.

The Hollywood Squares Season 2 finale on May 27, featuring Drew Barrymore as center square, will be the last CBS primetime episode of the season. Meanwhile, Marshals and Tracker closing out May 24 gives CBS a strong scripted send-off.

But CBS isn’t the only network that got an early start this year.

Fox’s Early Start and NBC’s Heavy Hitters

Fox broke from tradition by beginning its finale run in late March with Fear Factor: House of Fear. The network’s schedule stretches through spring:

  • March (late) — Fear Factor: House of Fear
  • April — Memory of a Killer, Best Medicine, Doc
  • May — Family Guy, Bob’s Burgers, Next Level Chef, The Masked Singer (2-hour finale)

The Masked Singer’s two-hour season finale remains Fox’s marquee unscripted event, where another celebrity will be unmasked and crowned champion. It’s become one of the most-watched finale events in recent broadcast history.

NBC has confirmed finale dates for several of its flagship franchises, including the One Chicago block (Chicago Fire, Chicago P.D., Chicago Med), the Law & Order trilogy (Law & Order, SVU, Organized Crime), and other returning favorites. Specific dates for some NBC shows are still being finalized, along with ABC’s America’s Funniest Home Videos and NBC’s Brilliant Minds (returning Wednesday, May 27) and Saturday Night Live.

Speaking of finales making headlines — one cable show just delivered a jaw-dropper.

Dark Winds Season 4 Finale: A Shocking Death Changes Everything

While broadcast networks map out their spring calendars, AMC’s Dark Winds just delivered one of the most talked-about finales of the year. The Season 4 finale aired on April 5–6, and it left fans reeling.

The finale served as a showcase for star Zahn McClarnon as Lieutenant Joe Leaphorn, who faced off against Irene Vaggan in a tense confrontation that closed out one of the season’s central cases. But it’s what happened next that has everyone talking.

The episode ended with a shocking murder that the show’s stars say will have major implications for Season 5. According to TV Insider, the cast broke down the death scene and teased what’s ahead, while Gold Derby reported that the “surprise death” could fundamentally reshape the show’s dynamics going forward.

The question on every fan’s mind: Is Gordo dead? Multiple outlets, including IMDb and Digital Mafia Talkies, published detailed recaps exploring the character’s fate and what it means for the series moving forward.

Dark Winds has established itself as one of the most critically acclaimed dramas on cable, and this finale only strengthens its case for awards consideration.

What This Means for Your Viewing Schedule

With over 60 finales compressed into roughly eight weeks, April and May 2026 demand serious planning. Here’s how to approach it:

  1. Mark the mega-nights. May 7 on ABC alone has three major drama finales airing simultaneously. CBS’s May 24 features both Marshals and Tracker.
  2. Say your goodbyes early. Watson (May 3), The Neighborhood (May 11), and DMV (May 11) are ending for good. If you’ve been a loyal viewer, these are your last episodes.
  3. Don’t overlook the cable gems. Dark Winds just proved that some of the best TV isn’t on the broadcast dial.
  4. Bookmark the schedule. Networks are still confirming dates for several shows. TVLine and TV Insider are regularly updating their comprehensive finale lists.

The 2026 finale season also carries extra weight because networks are simultaneously finalizing their 2026–2027 schedules. Some shows airing “season finales” may not get renewal announcements until after they’ve aired — meaning what looks like a season ender could quietly become a series finale.

Looking Ahead: The Summer Wait Begins

Finale season is always bittersweet. These episodes deliver the cliffhangers, resolutions, and emotional payoffs that make television worth watching — but they also mark the start of months without new episodes from your favorite shows.

With Scrubs back, Grey’s Anatomy still going strong in Season 22, and new hits like Marshals closing out their debut runs, the 2025–2026 broadcast season has been one of the most eventful in recent memory.

Which finale are you most anticipating — and which cancellation hit you the hardest? As networks reveal their fall 2026 lineups in the coming weeks, we’ll finally learn which returning shows earned another season and which said goodbye for good.
