Kathie Lee Gifford's Surprising Year of Pain and Resilience

July 1, 2026 00:03 Trend Insights

Kathie Lee Gifford says she feels “like Mr. Potato Head” — and at 72, the beloved TV legend has earned the right to joke about it.

In a candid new PEOPLE cover story, the former Today co-host pulled back the curtain on what may be the hardest year of her life: a cascade of surgeries, fractures, and chronic pain so severe she admits she “wanted to die.” Yet she greeted the interview the way she’s greeted five decades in the spotlight — cracking jokes and refusing to quit.

Quick Summary: Kathie Lee Gifford, 72, has endured hip replacement surgery, a re-fractured hip, a broken arm, another broken bone, and cataract surgery — all within roughly a year. Despite “debilitating” pain, she calls herself “a tough broad,” says she’s not interested in dating, and just listed her Connecticut estate for a staggering $100 million.

This isn’t a story about decline. It’s a story about how one of America’s most recognizable personalities keeps her sense of humor when her body keeps falling apart. Let’s start with what she’s actually been through.

The Surgeries Behind Kathie Lee Gifford’s Toughest Year

The list of injuries reads like a run of impossibly bad luck.

Just over a year ago, Kathie Lee Gifford underwent a total hip replacement. Then, while playing too energetically with her grandchildren, she fractured it again — requiring another surgery. She broke her arm one night simply by rolling over on it the wrong way. A spill on uneven pavement led to yet another broken bone. And when she realized her depth perception had gone off, she had cataract surgery on her eyes.

“I feel like Mr. Potato Head! One thing falls off and then another,” she told PEOPLE for the issue hitting newsstands this week.

Here’s the part that makes the story land: she never stopped laughing. Throughout a full day of a video interview at her primary residence and a photo shoot at her Provençal-style country retreat outside Nashville, she kept cracking good-natured jokes.

“But you have to have a sense of humor about everything,” she said. “Thank God I’ve never lost that, even in my bleakest moments. I’m a tough broad.”

That toughness didn’t come from nowhere. To understand it, you have to look at the punishing career that wore her body down in the first place.

A Career That Took a Physical Toll

Most fans remember Gifford for her warmth on morning television. Fewer realize how physically demanding her path to fame actually was.

Since her first big break on Name That Tune in 1977, Gifford says there have been “so many mountains I’ve climbed” as she moved “from one project to the next.” A lot of that work, she notes, “was physical.”

She points to one grueling example: playing Miss Hannigan in Annie at Madison Square Garden for five weeks over Christmastime in 2006. Years of performances, broadcasts, and projects added up to real wear and tear on her body.

Her resume is remarkable for its sheer length and range:

  • 1970s–1980s: Got her start in soap operas, appearing in Days of Our Lives and All My Children
  • 1985: Joined Regis Philbin on The Morning Show, later renamed Live! with Regis and Kathie Lee
  • 2000: Left Live! after 15 years
  • 2008: Joined Today, co-hosting the fourth hour with Hoda Kotb — famous for light-hearted humor and morning glasses of wine
  • 2019: Departed Today after a celebrated on-air farewell

A common misconception is that Gifford simply faded from view after leaving Today. The reality is the opposite — and it explains why she’s still trending today.

Where Kathie Lee Gifford Is Now

After signing off in 2019, Gifford didn’t retire. She returned to acting and writing, took on new projects, and threw herself into being a grandmother to her five grandchildren.

She also still swings by Today on occasion. When Hoda Kotb announced in September 2024 that she was leaving the show, Gifford sent a pre-taped video for Kotb’s January 2025 farewell episode — apologizing for being too “busy” to attend in person. It was a setup, of course: Gifford then popped out from backstage to surprise her former co-host.

It’s the kind of move that captures who she is — devoted to the people she loves, and never above a good prank.

That devotion runs deepest when it comes to her late husband, which brings us to the most personal question fans keep asking.

Will Kathie Lee Gifford Find Love Again?

Gifford was married to Frank Gifford — the legendary New York Giants player turned sports broadcaster — for 29 years before his death in 2015 at age 84.

In her latest interview, she was direct about her romantic life. “I am not dating anybody right now, and I don’t want to date anybody,” she said. “I’m not looking, and I don’t think I’ll ever get married again.”

She had previously said on Today in 2021 that she was in “a very special relationship” with a man she never named. By 2024, she told PEOPLE she was no longer dating that mystery man.

Still, she hasn’t completely closed the door:

“I leave room for the miraculous in my life. If God wants to do something miraculous to my life like give me another great love… Frank will always be the love of my life.”

Her larger philosophy may be the most quotable takeaway of all: “I need to find joy in the moment I’m in right now, because none of us know when is our last breath.”

What This Means for You

You don’t need to be a TV icon to take something practical from Gifford’s story.

ChallengeKathie Lee’s Approach
Chronic pain & setbacksKeep a sense of humor — “a tough broad” mindset
Career or life transitionPivot to new projects rather than retire
Loss of a partnerHonor the past while staying present
Uncertainty about the future“Leave room for the miraculous”

The actionable lesson is simple but powerful: resilience is a daily choice, not a personality trait you’re born with. Gifford faced multiple surgeries in a single year and still chose to laugh, work, and love her family. That’s a template anyone can borrow.

And in case you needed proof she’s still playing on a different level — she recently listed her waterfront Connecticut estate for $100 million, a number that made headlines across real estate and entertainment media alike.

The Bottom Line

Kathie Lee Gifford has spent nearly 50 years in front of cameras, and her latest chapter proves she’s as compelling as ever. A brutal run of injuries would sideline most people. Instead, she turned it into a story about humor, faith, and finding joy in the present.

So here’s the question worth sitting with: when life keeps knocking you down, do you brace for the next blow — or do you, like Kathie Lee, decide to be a “tough broad” and laugh anyway?

What would it take for you to find joy in the moment you’re in right now?
