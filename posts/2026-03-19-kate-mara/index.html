Kate Mara's Surprising New Roles: From 'Imperfect Women' to Werner Herzog

March 19, 2026 22:32 Trend Insights

What happens when Martha Stewart crashes your birthday party — and you end up inviting her to stay?

That’s the kind of delightfully unexpected moment that defines Kate Mara’s life right now. The 43-year-old actress is having a remarkable 2026, headlining Apple TV+’s buzzy new limited series Imperfect Women alongside Elisabeth Moss and Kerry Washington, while simultaneously preparing for a career milestone — her first-ever film with sister Rooney Mara, directed by none other than legendary filmmaker Werner Herzog.

Quick Summary

  • Kate Mara co-stars in Apple TV+’s Imperfect Women, a psychological thriller premiering March 2026
  • The series also features Elisabeth Moss, Kerry Washington, Leslie Odom Jr., and Corey Stoll
  • Kate and Rooney Mara will appear together for the first time in Werner Herzog’s Bucking Fastard
  • Martha Stewart unexpectedly crashed Kate’s birthday screening of Wuthering Heights
  • Reviews for Imperfect Women have been mixed but acknowledge the star power

Kate Mara in ‘Imperfect Women’: A Star-Studded Thriller

Apple TV+’s Imperfect Women is one of the most anticipated limited series of early 2026, and for good reason. The show brings together three of Hollywood’s most compelling actresses — Kate Mara, Elisabeth Moss, and Kerry Washington — in a psychological thriller adapted from a bestselling book.

The series premiered with a red carpet event on March 10 in Los Angeles, drawing an impressive lineup that also included Leslie Odom Jr. and Corey Stoll. Since then, the cast has been on a whirlwind promotional tour, appearing together in New York City and at various press events through mid-March.

So how has the show been received? The reviews tell an interesting story.

Critical Reception: Fun but Formulaic?

The Hollywood Reporter described Imperfect Women as a “maddeningly generic murder-among-the-wealthy thriller” that doesn’t fully utilize its exceptional cast. The review acknowledged the star power but felt the show fell into familiar territory.

The Guardian took a more forgiving stance, calling it “lots of fun… if you lower your expectations enough.” The implication: enjoy it as a guilty-pleasure thriller rather than prestige television.

What’s notable is that no one questions the performances. The criticism centers on the material, not the talent — a distinction that matters for Kate Mara’s career trajectory.

Despite mixed reviews, the show’s star-studded cast and Apple’s marketing muscle make it one of the most-discussed new series of March 2026. But Kate’s most exciting project may actually be the one she hasn’t released yet.

A Historic First: Kate and Rooney Mara Finally Share the Screen

Here’s a fact that surprises most people: despite both being established Hollywood actresses for over two decades, Kate and Rooney Mara have never appeared in a film together — until now.

The sisters will co-star as twin siblings Jean and Joan Holbrooke in Bucking Fastard, directed by the iconic Werner Herzog. The film is partially set in Ireland and follows twins who are so deeply connected that they “speak in unison, love the same man, and have the same dreams.”

According to the film’s synopsis, the twins embark on a quest for an imaginary land called the Orkneys — “where true love is possible” — and begin digging a tunnel through an entire mountain range to find it.

Speaking at the Imperfect Women premiere, Kate, 43, described the experience of filming with Rooney, 40, in deeply personal terms.

“It was very special,” she told People exclusively. “The script is unlike anything we’ve ever read before. We’re basically playing the same person.”

She added that working together actually brought them closer: “We didn’t want it to end. The experience was unlike anything else that I’ve ever had.”

The combination of the Mara sisters and Werner Herzog — a filmmaker known for pushing artistic boundaries — has generated significant industry buzz. For fans who have waited years to see the sisters act together, this project promises to be worth the wait.

But between press tours and filming, Kate also found time for one of the most entertaining celebrity anecdotes of the year.

Martha Stewart Crashes Kate Mara’s Birthday Party

During a March 17 appearance on Late Night with Seth Meyers, Kate shared a hilarious story about her birthday celebration that quickly went viral.

Kate had planned a low-key birthday: renting out a small local theater to watch Wuthering Heights with just five friends. Simple, intimate, perfect.

Then Martha Stewart entered the picture.

“On the day of my birthday,” Kate recalled, “I got an email from the theater saying, ‘Oh my gosh we’re so embarrassed, we have a new employee, and they got a call from Martha Stewart’s assistant saying Martha would love to rent out the theater tonight to watch Wuthering Heights.’”

The coincidence is almost too perfect — both Kate Mara and Martha Stewart wanting to rent the same small theater on the same night to watch the same film.

The new employee, unaware of the existing booking, initially told Martha’s team the theater was already reserved by Kate Mara. What happened next showcased Kate’s gracious personality.

Rather than simply asserting her reservation, Kate extended an invitation. “We’ll sit in the back row,” she offered, welcoming Martha to join the birthday screening.

The anecdote captures something essential about Kate Mara — a down-to-earth quality that coexists with her Hollywood pedigree (the Mara family owns both the New York Giants and Pittsburgh Steelers NFL franchises).

What’s Next for Kate Mara in 2026

With Imperfect Women now streaming and Bucking Fastard in the pipeline, Kate Mara is in an enviable position. She’s balancing mainstream visibility through a major Apple TV+ series with artistic ambition through a Werner Herzog collaboration.

The promotional tour for Imperfect Women has taken her and co-stars Kerry Washington and Elisabeth Moss from Los Angeles to New York in recent weeks, with appearances at the SAG-AFTRA Foundation and multiple press events generating constant buzz.

What makes Kate Mara’s 2026 particularly compelling is the range on display. In Imperfect Women, she’s part of an ensemble navigating wealth, secrets, and murder. In Bucking Fastard, she’s exploring surrealist territory with one of cinema’s most daring directors — and doing it alongside her own sister for the first time.

For an actress who first gained widespread attention as a doomed reporter in House of Cards more than a decade ago, the evolution is striking. Kate Mara isn’t just working — she’s choosing projects that challenge her and create moments audiences will remember.

Which raises a question worth considering: in an era of franchise filmmaking and safe bets, isn’t it refreshing to see an actress bet on Werner Herzog and a story about twins digging through mountains?
