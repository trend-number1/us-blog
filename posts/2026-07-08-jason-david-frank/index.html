Jason David Frank's Final Film Finally Arrives

July 8, 2026 12:03 Trend Insights

Nearly four years after his death, Jason David Frank is about to appear on the big screen one last time — and the just-released trailer for his final film is stirring emotions across the Power Rangers fandom.

The late actor, who became a genre legend as the original Green Ranger, stars in Legend of the White Dragon, a project years in the making that finally heads to theaters this summer. For fans, it’s a bittersweet moment: a chance to see their hero suit up one more time.

Quick Summary: Jason David Frank (1973–2022) played Tommy Oliver, the Green and White Ranger, in Mighty Morphin Power Rangers. His final film, Legend of the White Dragon, releases in theaters on August 28, with a new trailer arriving in July 2026. Frank died by suicide in November 2022 at age 49.

Who Was Jason David Frank?

Jason David Frank (September 4, 1973 – November 19, 2022) was an American actor and mixed martial artist, best known for playing Tommy Oliver across the Power Rangers television franchise.

Born in Covina, California, Frank worked as a child model for a company called Olive Productions during the 1980s. In 1992, he even appeared as a contestant on The Price Is Right — a small footnote before fame arrived.

His big break came when he was cast as Tommy Oliver, the Green Ranger, in Mighty Morphin Power Rangers. Here’s the surprising part: the role was only meant to last 14 episodes.

But audiences fell in love with the character. Due to the Green Ranger’s runaway popularity, producers brought Frank back — this time as the White Ranger and the team’s new leader. It’s one of TV’s clearest examples of fans directly reshaping a show.

Frank went on to reprise Tommy in Power Rangers Zeo, Turbo, and Dino Thunder, making him one of the most enduring faces in the franchise’s history. So how did a 14-episode gig become a lifelong legacy?

The Roles That Almost Were

Frank’s career is full of near-misses that reveal just how central he became to 1990s pop culture.

A huge fan of professional wrestling, Frank caught the attention of WWF’s Vince McMahon, who wanted the Power Rangers cast to appear on WWF programming to promote Mighty Morphin Power Rangers: The Movie. McMahon even wanted Frank to referee the famous casket match between The Undertaker and Yokozuna at Survivor Series.

There was just one problem. Power Rangers creator Haim Saban refused to allow the franchise on WWF television. Frank was replaced in that role by none other than Chuck Norris.

That wasn’t his only alternate path. Consider this list of what nearly happened:

  • Frank was originally set to play lead character Adam Steele in VR Troopers (originally titled Cybertron) and even shot a pilot episode.
  • He was called back to Power Rangers before that show launched.
  • According to Frank and fellow actor Brad Hawkins, Hawkins’ character was initially meant to replace Tommy Oliver as the White Ranger.

His other on-screen credits included episodes of Sweet Valley High and Family Matters. But it was always Tommy Oliver who defined him — and that connection makes his final project all the more poignant.

Inside “Legend of the White Dragon”

Frank’s last film has been a long journey. Legend of the White Dragon became a reality after a highly successful Kickstarter campaign in 2020, and it’s been in the works ever since.

Directed by Aaron Schoenke, who co-wrote the screenplay with Alex Kellerman, the movie is produced by Bat in the Sun and distributed by Well Go USA Entertainment. A new trailer landed in early July 2026, offering the clearest look yet at the action-packed superhero adventure.

Here’s the key thing to understand: the film isn’t connected to the Power Rangers franchise. Instead, it channels the same larger-than-life energy — colorful characters, tokusatsu-inspired style, and gravity-defying martial arts action — that made Frank a genre icon in the first place.

In the movie, Frank stars as Erik Reed, a once-celebrated hero known as the White Dragon. After taking responsibility for a devastating tragedy that ended his career, Reed spends years living in the shadows. When the ruthless Dragon Prime — played by director Schoenke — threatens everything he has left, Reed is forced back into action to reclaim his reputation and protect his family and legacy.

It’s a fitting final bow — one built entirely around the martial-arts hero persona Frank spent decades perfecting. But the release also arrives against a deeply tragic backdrop.

A Life Marked by Both Triumph and Tragedy

Frank’s story is not without heartbreak. He died by suicide on November 19, 2022, at the age of 49, a loss that stunned fans worldwide.

His wife, Tammie Frank, later spoke openly about the circumstances. “I had no idea that Jason was thinking of ending things,” she said. The couple had recently separated following the death of her daughter Shayla, whom Jason had helped raise. “Between losing her and helping raise her baby son, Jason and I started having marital issues,” Tammie explained.

At the time of his passing, however, the two had called off their separation and were working on reuniting. He died during what was meant to be a short vacation. “It was meant to be a fun weekend getaway, and instead, I lost the love of my life,” Tammie said. “Jason was a good man, but he was not without his demons. He was human, just like the rest of us.”

In later years, Frank had struggled with addiction and deep emotional turmoil, particularly after his brother, Erik Frank — who appeared in Power Rangers Zeo as Tommy’s long-lost brother David Trueheart — died by suicide in 2001 at age 29.

What this means for fans: Frank’s legacy is being preserved in tangible ways. In June 2026, a new mural honoring him was unveiled at an Oviedo, Florida academy, and his final film ensures a new generation will discover the hero he embodied.

The Enduring Legacy of the Green Ranger

Few actors so completely fuse with a single character. For millions who grew up in the 1990s, Jason David Frank was the Green Ranger — the mysterious warrior who arrived as a villain and became the franchise’s beating heart.

Legend of the White Dragon is more than a movie; it’s a final gift from an artist to the community that adored him. It captures everything that made him special: the discipline of a real martial artist, the charisma of a leading man, and the genuine love he had for his fans.
