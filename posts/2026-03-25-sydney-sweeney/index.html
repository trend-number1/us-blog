Sydney Sweeney's Unstoppable 2026: Housemaid Sequel, SYRN Lingerie, and Hollywood Controversy

March 25, 2026 06:03 Trend Insights

Sydney Sweeney just secured one of Hollywood’s most iconic actresses for her next blockbuster — and simultaneously sparked a firestorm over bras draped across the Hollywood sign. If 2025 was her breakout year, 2026 is shaping up to be the year she becomes truly untouchable.

Key Takeaways

  • Kirsten Dunst has been cast opposite Sydney Sweeney in The Housemaid’s Secret, the sequel to the nearly $400 million worldwide hit
  • Director Paul Feig and screenwriter Rebecca Sonnenshine are both returning
  • Sweeney’s lingerie brand SYRN made headlines with a daring Hollywood sign publicity stunt — done without authorization
  • The Hollywood Chamber of Commerce publicly condemned the stunt
  • Sweeney continues expanding her empire as both actress and producer through Fifty-Fifty Films

Kirsten Dunst Joins The Housemaid’s Secret

The biggest Sydney Sweeney news this week is the casting of Kirsten Dunst in The Housemaid’s Secret, the highly anticipated sequel to 2025’s The Housemaid. Lionsgate confirmed the announcement on March 23, 2026.

The first film was a global smash, earning nearly $400 million worldwide. That kind of box office muscle makes the sequel one of the most anticipated projects in Hollywood right now.

Dunst will portray Mrs. Wendy Garrick, the reclusive new employer to Sweeney’s character Millie Calloway. Lionsgate Motion Picture Group President Erin Westerman didn’t hold back in her praise:

“She is an icon. Her career reflects extraordinary range and fearlessness. Opposite the ever-magnetic Sydney Sweeney, she will be an electrifying force in a world where nothing is ever quite as it seems.”

The sequel is based on Freida McFadden’s bestselling novel of the same name. In the story, Millie returns to take a housekeeping job for a woman she’s never allowed to see — only to uncover secrets far darker than her own.

This is also a big week for Dunst, who landed a second major role: she’ll play Alex in the sequel to A Minecraft Movie. Her most recent film credit was Roofman, a crime comedy alongside Channing Tatum.

So who else is coming back for round two?

Sweeney isn’t just starring — she’s producing through her Fifty-Fifty Films banner, reinforcing her position as one of Hollywood’s most powerful young producers. Original author Freida McFadden has expressed one particular wish for the sequel: a cameo from Amanda Seyfried, who starred in the first film alongside Brendan Sklenar.

“Even though she’s not in the movie, I’m hoping Amanda Seyfried, if she’s free, can at least do a cameo,” McFadden told E! News. “She was such a star in the movie.”

But Sweeney’s ambitions extend far beyond the Housemaid franchise.

SYRN Lingerie: A 44-Size Revolution Meets Hollywood Scandal

Sweeney’s entrepreneurial side has been making just as many headlines as her acting career. Her lingerie brand SYRN by Sydney Sweeney has positioned itself as an inclusive label, offering an impressive 44 sizes — a move ELLE called “a revolution” in the intimates space.

The brand launched in early 2026 and has kept up a steady drumbeat of attention. Sweeney has appeared in multiple promotional campaigns, including looks described as “unexpectedly daring” by fashion outlets and a lacy bodysuit with a major cut-out featured across Yahoo, Page Six, and the Daily Star.

But it was a nighttime publicity stunt at the Hollywood sign that pushed SYRN from fashion news into hard news.

The Hollywood Sign Incident

On March 24, 2026, Sweeney shared an Instagram video showing herself and a camera crew hiking up to the Hollywood sign under cover of darkness. They scaled Mount Lee, unzipped duffel bags full of bras, and draped them over the iconic letters.

In the video, Sweeney can be heard saying, “I’m gonna get caught at this rate.”

The backlash was swift. The Hollywood Chamber of Commerce, which owns trademark rights for commercial use of the sign, released a statement confirming:

  • Sweeney and her team did not obtain permission for the stunt
  • The Chamber had no prior knowledge of the action
  • The stunt was not authorized for commercial purposes

The LAPD told ABC News that no police report had been filed against the actress. Still, the incident raised questions about where bold marketing ends and disrespect for public landmarks begins.

It’s the kind of controversy that generates enormous attention — which, for a new brand, may be exactly the point.

Sydney Sweeney’s Expanding Empire: What 2025 Built

To understand Sweeney’s current momentum, you have to look at 2025. It was a year of extraordinary highs and notable lows.

Career highlights in 2025:

  • Multiple film premieres: Echo Valley, Eden, Americana, Christy, and The Housemaid
  • Returned to film Euphoria Season 3 (the final season)
  • Continued building Fifty-Fifty Films as a production company

Personal changes:

  • Her engagement to Jonathan Davino ended, with Us Weekly confirming the split
  • She was spotted out with Anyone But You costar Glen Powell in Dallas, though sources described them as “just” friends

Controversies:

  • Faced backlash over unconventional brand deals, including a bath-water themed soap promotion
  • Some film projects underperformed at the box office

Despite the setbacks, Sweeney emerged from 2025 more in-demand than ever. Her 2026 slate reflects an artist who has learned to leverage both controversy and talent into sustained cultural relevance.

Reports also indicate she has joined a Netflix live-action adaptation of a popular sci-fi anime franchise, co-starring with a Spider-Man star — further diversifying her portfolio across studios and genres.

What This Means for Hollywood’s Next Power Player

Sydney Sweeney’s trajectory tells us something important about modern celebrity. She’s not just an actress waiting for roles — she’s a producer, brand founder, and marketing provocateur all at once.

Here’s what to watch:

  • The Housemaid’s Secret production timeline and additional casting announcements
  • Whether the Hollywood sign stunt results in any legal or regulatory consequences
  • SYRN’s sales trajectory after the wave of attention
  • Her Netflix anime adaptation, which could open an entirely new fan base
  • Euphoria Season 3’s reception and how it shapes her legacy from the show that made her famous

At just 28, Sweeney is building the kind of multi-platform empire that used to take decades. Whether you admire the hustle or question the methods, one thing is clear: Sydney Sweeney isn’t slowing down, and 2026 might be her biggest year yet.
